1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide through contact forms, account registration, purchases, and newsletter subscriptions. This may include your name, email address, account credentials, transaction details, communications, and subscription preferences.

We may also collect technical and usage information, including IP addresses, browser and device information, pages visited, referring URLs, cookie identifiers, and Website interactions.

Location-related information may be collected through IP addresses or device and browser permissions, depending on the Website's configuration and enabled features.

2. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Website.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process purchases and related transactions.
  • Respond to inquiries and contact forms.
  • Send newsletters and manage subscriptions.
  • Analyze Website usage and performance.
  • Measure advertising and retargeting campaigns.
  • Prevent fraud and protect Website security.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Analytics

The Website uses or may use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics, and advertising-related purposes.

We use Google Analytics to understand Website traffic, visitor interactions, and performance. Depending on the configuration, Google Analytics may collect information about your device, browser, IP address, and Website activity.

Learn more about Google's practices at Google's Privacy Policy .

You may manage certain cookies through your browser settings and any cookie preference tools provided on the Website. Where required by law, we will obtain consent before using nonessential cookies or similar technologies.

4. How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information to service providers that support Website hosting, payment processing, analytics, email delivery, advertising, security, and other business operations.

We may also disclose information when required or permitted by law, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transaction.

5. Advertising and Opt-Out Rights

The Website uses or may use advertising pixels and retargeting technologies. These technologies may allow advertising partners to receive information such as online identifiers, IP addresses, browsing activity, and interactions with advertisements.

Certain disclosures may constitute a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising under applicable state privacy laws, even when no money is exchanged.

To request an opt-out, email privacy@robintunneyofficial.com with the subject "Privacy Opt-Out Request."

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate on-site opt-out mechanisms and honor qualifying opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control.

6. Newsletters and Email Communications

We use Mailchimp to provide newsletter and email marketing services. If you subscribe, your email address and related subscription information may be processed by Mailchimp to deliver and manage communications.

You may unsubscribe using the link included in marketing emails or contact us at privacy@robintunneyofficial.com .

Review Intuit's Privacy Statement for more information about Mailchimp's provider privacy practices.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage accounts, complete transactions, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our rights.

When information is no longer necessary, we will delete, de-identify, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with applicable law, subject to lawful retention exceptions.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your U.S. Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of certain personal information, as well as opt out of certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling.

You may submit a privacy request by emailing privacy@robintunneyofficial.com .

We may verify requests where permitted or required by law and will respond within applicable legal timeframes. Additional rights, exceptions, and appeal procedures may apply depending on your state.

California Residents

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, applies to our business, California residents may have additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, and opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Website account by emailing privacy@robintunneyofficial.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request."

We may verify your identity before processing the request. Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law, including for transaction records, legal compliance, security, and fraud prevention.

Account deletion does not automatically delete information that independent third-party providers are legally permitted or required to retain.

11. Children's Privacy

The Website is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed to target children. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13 without complying with applicable legal requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Website, contact privacy@robintunneyofficial.com .
